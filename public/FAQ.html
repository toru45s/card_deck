Frequently Asked Questions

What if I need more time after the free trial?

You can continue using the free decks included even after the trial period is over. Additionally, you can choose an extra low-cost monthly subscription for individual decks. You may cancel the subscription at any time, so your risk is minimal! 

How can I pay?

You can pay using any major credit card or Paypal. Paypal handles the payment so you know that you are in good hands. For institutional orders, please contact us by phone (+972-3-3761175) or mail (sales@cardtherapy.online).

Do I need to give credit card details to try?

No. Just go to the signup page, enter your email and choose a user name and password. You can also register using your Google or Facebook account.  

Is there free content on the site?

Yes! Even after your trial period expires, there are a number of decks that are free to use. We periodically update the list - so just jump inand have a look.
