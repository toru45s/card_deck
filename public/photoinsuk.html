Tell me a story

Choose 5-9 cards. Tell as story using the cards.
In what way is the person in the story similar to you? In what is he or she different?

Could the story end differently? What would need to change for the new ending to happen?

Photo Card Deck

There are two more backgrounds in the pack that can be used:

1) Bull's eye

2) Matrix

Bull's Eye: Using the bull's eye background, we represent a current situation and where you like to aim for.

For example, choose 5 cards that represent a relationship, and then place them on the bull's eye. The center represents where you want to be and the farther away from the center – the farther you are from where you want to be.

The matrix can be used for sorting. Sorting helps us become aware and identifying different issues. You can use the Matrix background and sort by different categories. For example place cards in different quadrants that represent:

  1. What is important to you
  2. Feelings, thoughts, feelings or memories [internal things] that appear when you consider what is important.
  3. What do you do to avoid these feelings / thoughts / feelings
  4. How will you react - when these thoughts / feelings / beliefs will not affect you.

Create a vision board for yourself!

What is a vision? A vision is an image that a person sees in his mind's eye, in his imagination, in his dream.

Vision is where we want to go. The best place we can imagine for ourselves in all aspects of life or in specific areas. In order to reach it, it is worth first of all ... to define it, in a clear and unambiguous way, to really see in our mind's eye our best imagination-

How does it look? What will be there? What will our house look like? How will our meaningful relationships be? What assets will we have (spiritual, personal, economic).

Imagine that you were transported into the your vision. Describe in as much detail as possible and once in a while update it.

When you have a vision, it is easier to find the ways to get there! A vision can provide the motivation and fuel for the long journey.

Choose values, a quote and a picture that describe the place you want to be and the way you want to get there

You could print your vision board and hang it up in a visible place for a daily reminder 😊
