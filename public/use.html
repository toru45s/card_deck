How to use the cards

Therapeutic cards allow a new dimension for the therapist and patient. No need to download cards, the site opens quickly. Allows you to work together on the same screen remotely, easily! Even if you are really afraid of technological tools, you will be surprised to find out how simple and pleasant the work is.

The sessions are saved on the site and there is no need to save in another place! You can print the work products, save them as a PDF and print the cards and playing area.
The tools we have created for the community of therapists and coaches are now availabe for a free 30 day trial.

D.W. Winnicott said that "Playing is an experience, always a creative experience, and it is an experience in the space-time continuum, a basic form of living"   

Playing is therapy, let's get started!
