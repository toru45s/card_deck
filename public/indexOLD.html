What is Digi Card Therapy?

Digi Card Therapy offers effective tools for facilitating personal development, increased awareness and focus on internal work processes in a dynamic and playful way. The site combines tools, ideas and different ways of working with a vairiety of different therapy cards. The available cards decks include values, questionnaires, quotes, pictures, resilience and many more.
Digi Card Therapy was developed for remote work using platforms such as Zoom, Microsoft Teams and all other video conferencing software.

The tools we have developed for therapists and coaches are now available to try out for a month free of charge

During this complex period, remote treatments have become routine. Remote therapy and coaching is extremely challenging and adds various complexities for therapists and coaches, as well as for patients and clients.

Digi Card Therapy was designed specfically to provide digital therapeutic and coaching tools. Tools created specifically for remote care, and developed specifically for the unique needs of professionals. Although digital therapy cards were deisgned for online therapy and coaching, they are also extremely practical and easy to use during sessions in the clinic, saving time and space.
